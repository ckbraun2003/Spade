Findings (Physics):
1. Loads and stores keep subnormals. Every arithmetic op flushes, as a sign-preserving FTZ+DAZ: add, sub, mul and div on inputs and outputs; abs, neg, min, max, clamp, compares and sqrt on inputs. -denorm < 0 is false; abs(-denorm) = +0; max(denorm, 0) = 0; sqrt(denorm) = 0.
2. NoContraction is honoured: 0 of 27,000 mul-then-add cases match std::fma, including the (1+2^-12)^2 - 1 cases. The product is flushed on its own before the add.
3. floor and float->int keep subnormals: floor(-denorm) = -1. The cell flip comes one step earlier: pos/cell_size is a division, a subnormal quotient flushes to -0, floor gives -0, and the cell is 0, not -1. Only for |pos| < 2^-126 * cell_size. LEAD WITH THIS, phrased that way.
4. Not denormals: NVIDIA fp32 div and sqrt are not correctly rounded on NORMAL operands (within Vulkan's allowance). 8 div cases and 1 sqrt case are 1 ulp off. (1+2^-12)/3 gives 0x3EAAB556 against host 0x3EAAB555; sqrt(1.5*2^-126) gives 0x201CC470 against 0x201CC471. A denormal fix does not remove these parity differences.
5. M2's log32(denorm_min) fits finding 1: log32's pre-scale multiplies a subnormal, and the multiply flushes its input.
