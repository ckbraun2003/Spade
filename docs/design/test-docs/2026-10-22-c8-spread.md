# C8's spread under the CPU's last bits, and a measure that holds across it

**Owner:** Test/Docs. **Asked by:** the lead, 2026-10-22, after Rendering's B4 finding (`e965df2`; C8's record, `tests/data/airflow/records/c8-actuator-disc.md`). **Status:** measured; §3 signed off by Physics (§6). The re-cut reaches Physics' test and record, so it waits for the lead's grant for the files in §6.

## 1. The ensemble

- **The harness:** `tests/test_airflow_c8_spread.cpp` on `test-docs/c8-spread` (`40c3462`). It runs C8's case unchanged, on the CPU, MSVC 19.43 release.
  - Each run puts 110 of the disc's 1020 increments 1 ulp up, the same every step. This is B4's nudge: 110 is how many increments the GPU gather differs on.
  - It records six probes after every step:
    - the downwash on the axis at R, 2R, 3R and 4R below the disc (C8's four-face mean; 2R is C8's own probe);
    - the mean downwash over the v faces within 0.5R (80 faces) and within R (316) of the axis, 2R below.
- **The members:** 16, each 768 steps long, twice C8's 384.
  - Member 0 is unnudged. Members 1 to 15 take seeds 0xC8 to 0xD6.
  - Four run at once, one thread each, at 311 to 320 s per four. Queue tickets `spade-testdocs-c8ens-{a,b,c,d}-40c3462`.
- **The check:** member 0's C8 measure is 7.412388 m/s, C8's record. Members 1 to 3 give 7.479177, 7.058580 and 6.613182, B4's three runs, digit for digit.

The references are momentum theory on the axis, `v_h (1 + z / √(z² + R²))`: 7.5777 m/s at 2R and 7.8806 at 4R. The percentages are of those, and the sd is the members' standard deviation over the reference.

- **C8's verdict is a coin with a bias.** On C8's own measure, 3 of 16 trajectories miss the 10%. The first record's "within" was one of the 13.
- **The horizon `T_p`:**
  - Under the banded-parity plan's rule (1e-5 of the scene's scale, 7.6e-5 m/s at 7.58 m/s), the earliest member leaves member 0 on the axis at 2R at step 104 (0.13 s). The median is 109.
  - The members part by 1e-3 relative at steps 141 to 168 (B4's "about 140"), and by 1e-5 m/s at steps 60 to 67.
  - The 0.5R disc parts at step 96 under the rule (median 116), and by 1e-3 relative at steps 158 to 254.
- **No window before `T_p` can carry the validation.** The wake at 2R is not developed by then: the axis reads 6.53 m/s over steps 97 to 128, −13.9%. A window before `T_p` serves only the GPU's band (§4).
- **Why the axis is the chaotic probe.** `spread_actuator` loads the disc at 48 points: 3 rings of 16, at `R √((ring + ½) / 3)`. The inner ring sits at 0.408R, 4.08 cells. Peskin's kernel reaches 2 cells along each axis, so no column of faces within 2.1 cells (0.21R) of the axis gets any thrust: 12 columns at R/dx 10. The disc has an unloaded hub. C8's four faces sit 0.07R off the axis, in that hub's wake: a slow core inside a fast annulus, with a shear layer about 2 cells out that rolls up and meanders.
  - The 0.5R mean spans both the hub and the inner ring's annulus, so the meander averages out. Its sd is a tenth of the axis's: 0.38% against 4.86% in the same window.
  - The time-mean profile dips on the axis: over steps 385 to 768 the 0.5R mean is 10% over the axis's (8.02 against 7.27 m/s). Momentum theory's uniform slipstream has no dip. So the axis probe reads low (−4% to −7%), and the 0.5R mean reads high (+6% to +7%).
  - The likely cause of the +6% is the 48-point loading. Inside 0.5R it carries 1.34× a uniform disc's thrust per area: none within 2.1 cells, 1.7 to 1.9× at 3 to 4 cells, and half at 5.
  - Annulus momentum theory would put the local velocity at √1.34 = 1.16× momentum theory's. Mixing across the rings pulls that down, and the measured +6% sits between the two.
  - Whether the +6% wants a note is Physics' call. It is inside the 10%, with 3.4% to spare at the worst member.
- **The 0.5R disc settles by step 385.** Its 64-step block means, over all 16 members, are 8.13 to 8.16 m/s through step 384, and 7.99 to 8.05 from step 385 on.
- **The disc of R is not a momentum-theory measure.** At 2R the slipstream has contracted to about 0.73R (`R √(v_h / v)`), so the disc of R averages the slipstream with the still air around it.

## 3. The proposal, fixed before the run that judges it (`TD-2`)

- **The measure:** the mean downwash over the v faces within 0.5R of the axis, 2R below the disc (80 faces at R/dx 10, chosen by their centres), averaged over steps 385 to 768.
  - The run doubles to 768 steps (0.96 s). The window is its second half, past the settling in §2.
  - It is the probe the end-goal checks already read: a disc of 0.5R, 2R below each hub (`kProbeLatticeHalf`, `kProbePitchR`, `sandbox/end_goal_checks.hpp`). So C8 would validate the measure the editor's downwash checks use.
- **The reference:** momentum theory's slipstream velocity 2R below, `v_h (1 + 2/√5)` = 7.5777 m/s, unchanged. In momentum theory the slipstream's velocity is uniform across a station. The vortex-cylinder model's mean over the same 80 faces is 7.5909, 0.17% over it, and the record states that.
- **The tolerance:** within 10%, unchanged (plan A Task 11, spec §11.2). The spread never widens it (01-verification, "A case judged past its horizon").
- **The verdict is the ensemble's:** eight runs, the banded-parity plan's eight. Member 0 is unnudged; the other seven are nudged as in §1, from seeds this measurement did not use (0x1C8 to 0x1CE). The record prints four measures:
  1. the ensemble's mean: within 10%;
  2. the ensemble's worst member: within 10%, so the verdict holds across the spread;
  3. the ensemble's largest deviation from its mean: report only (the GPU's band is 4× it, §4);
  4. C8's old measure (the axis 2R, steps 257 to 384), member 0's value and the ensemble's range: report only.
- **What this ensemble predicts:** a mean of about +5.9%, a worst member of about +6.6%, and a largest deviation of about 0.05 m/s. Any 8 of the 16 give a mean of +5.6% to +6.2%, and a worst member of 6.1% to 6.6%.
- **The cost:** 8 runs of 768 steps, in two batches of four at about 320 s each: about 11 minutes, about five times C8's one run alone (2.3 minutes, B4's timing). It stays opt-in, as C8 is.
- **The first record's block stays,** read as the one run it was (the 2026-10-22 note below it).

## 4. The GPU

The rule is in 01-verification, "A case judged past its horizon" (`6b77cbc`). For C8:
- **Up to `T_p`** (step 104 under the rule; the members agree to 1e-5 m/s through step 60): the probe series is banded against the CPU's run, per `TD-14`.
- **Past `T_p`:** the GPU's 0.5R measure must be within 10% of momentum theory. It must also be within 4× the ensemble's largest deviation of the ensemble's mean: this ensemble gives 4 × 0.054 = 0.21 m/s, 2.7%.
- **B4's "GPU gather in the CPU step" test** asserts that the gather's run parts no sooner than the earliest nudge's. That holds against all 16 members: their earliest 1e-3 parting is step 141, and the gather's is 169.
