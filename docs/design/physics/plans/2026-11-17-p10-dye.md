# P10: the passive dye (the website's smoke and dye volumes)

Cameron, 2026-11-17 (L86h): "Both: volume now, smoke later". This is the solver half: a passive tracer carried by the
air, released at the rotors, published into the flow volume's fourth channel (V's channel 3, `kFlowDye`), where
`flow_render` draws it through a dye or smoke transfer function. The proof (L86g: CPU only, 1 cm grid, stills at the S2
camera) is on `physics/dye-proof`; this is the build.

## What it is

One float per CELL, `d` in [0, 1], 0 clean air. Per step, after the flow's step:

1. **Advect** (`advect_scalar`, advect.cpp; the GPU twin `airflow_dye_advect`): an RK2 trace of the cell centre through the
   step's final face velocity, semi-Lagrangian, and the MacCormack correction clamped to the range of the eight cells the
   trace interpolated. So every new value lies in [min, max] of its departure stencil: never negative, no new extremum
   (a maximum principle). It is diffusive at the stencil scale and not conservative: a plume thins as it travels. A trace
   that leaves the box clamps to the edge cell, as the velocity's does.
2. **Release** (`emit_dye`, dye.cpp; `airflow_dye_source`): each disc adds `d <- d + (1 - d) inc` over a sheet DOWNSTREAM of
   its plane, `inc = min(1, rate h 2 phi4(s / dx) w(r))`, `s >= 0` the cell centre's distance along the disc's axis (so
   the halo the proof showed above the disc, the 4-point kernel straddling the plane, is gone: the release is one-sided,
   the 2 keeps its amount), `phi4` Peskin's 4-point kernel (2 cells deep), and `w(r)` a window one cell wide at each edge
   between `r_in R` and `r_out R`. The web look is the tip ring, r/R 0.85-1.0 (`DyeRelease`'s default), rate 120/s; the
   whole disc is `r_in = 0`.
3. **Publish**: V's channel 3 is `d` (a solid cell's is the mean of its fluid neighbours', as every channel's is).

PASSIVE: the dye never acts on the flow. With it off, or on, u, v, w and p are the same bits (tests on the CPU and the
device). Nothing here moves a bit of a run that does not enable it.

## Where it lives

- CPU: `FluidScratch::dye` (+ `enable_dye()`), advected at the end of `fluid_step()`; the caller releases with
  `emit_dye()` after the step, as it calls `spread_actuator()` before it. `PublishInput::dye`.
- GPU: three NEW kernels, so no existing SpirvPin row moves: `airflow_dye_advect` (two passes, scratch in
  `airflow_advect`), `airflow_dye_source` (reads the discs' records, word 11 = the radius, which the gather kernel
  ignores; also copies next to cur), `airflow_dye_publish` (overwrites channel 3 after `airflow_publish_fields`, with its
  solid-neighbour mean). One new binding, `airflow_dye` at 48 (`[0,4)` the release's k = rate h, r_in, r_out; then cur;
  then next), which grows set 1's number space by that one slot (49). `GpuFluid::create(..., const DyeSetup*)`,
  `read_dye()`. The chain runs after the step (after the solids' projection), once per disc count.
- Tests: `tests/test_airflow_dye.cpp` (CPU: the maximum principle, uniform stays uniform to the bit, one cell a step at
  Courant 1, the release's bounds, one-sidedness and ring, the flow's bits with the dye on or off, the publish; the proof
  stills opt-in), `tests/test_gpu_airflow_dye.cpp` (the device is the CPU bit for bit on three cases and at workgroup
  32, 64 and 128, the publish with solids, the flow's bits with the dye, refusals; the deep-box cost opt-in).
  Nine SpirvPin rows (3 kernels x 32, 64, 128).

## What it is not

Not smoke physics: no buoyancy, no combustion, no diffusion beyond the scheme's own. Not conservative. Not a particle
tracer: it cannot show a streakline through a vortex core that the 4 mm grid does not resolve. The dye in a solid cell is
carried by the same scheme (it is not masked) but is never published (the solid's value is its neighbours' mean).

## Cost

Measured 2026-11-17 on the RTX 3060 Ti in the web clips' deep box (kD1DeepGrid 240 x 392 x 168 = 15.8 M cells, the quad's
solids, h 0.2 ms, Cs 0.17, the four discs releasing the tip ring), `GpuAirflowDye.TheDyeCostsAStepInTheDeepBox`
(SPADE_DYE_COST=1), the mean of 30 steps after 2 warm ones:

| | ms a step | device memory |
|---|---|---|
| without the dye | 285.6 | 3194 MiB |
| with the dye | 291.5 (+5.9, +2.1%) | 3317 MiB (+122) |

The dye chain's own phase is 5.0 ms (the sizing said about 5 ms and 126 MB). Run to run the step moves by a few ms; the
difference is the dye's chain, not noise at that scale.
