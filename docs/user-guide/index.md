# Quick start

Spade Editor lets you put drones and objects in a scene, fly a drone with your keyboard, and watch the air it stirs up. This page gets you flying in five minutes.

You need Windows 10 or 11 and the installer file you were given.

## 1. Install it (1 minute)

1. Double-click the installer (its name starts with **Spade-Editor** and ends with **setup.exe**).
2. Click **Next** until it says **Install**, then **Install**, then **Finish**.

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. The full steps are in [Install and uninstall](install.md).

## 2. Open the editor (30 seconds)

1. Press the **Windows key**, type **Spade Editor** and press **Enter**.
2. The editor opens. A row of buttons is on top. A list called **Hierarchy** is on the left. The 3D view is in the middle, with one small drone in it. A panel called **Inspector** is on the right. The **Library** is along the bottom.

![The editor when it opens](images/03_editor.png)

## 3. Put a drone in the scene (1 minute)

1. In the **Library** at the bottom, find the row called **drones** and click the picture named **Phantom 3-class quad**. Its frame lights up, and a line under the buttons says "Click in the view to place Phantom 3-class quad."
2. Click on the grey ground in the 3D view, a little in front of the small drone.
3. The new drone appears where you clicked. Its name appears in the **Hierarchy** and it is selected.

## 4. Fly it (2 minutes)

1. Click **Run**, the first button on the top left. The **Inspector** shows a **Pilot** section that says "Flying phantom3_class, disarmed".
2. Press <kbd>Space</kbd>. It now says "armed" and the rotors start to turn.
3. Hold <kbd>Page Up</kbd>. The **Throttle** number in the **Pilot** section goes up and the drone climbs.
4. Press the arrow keys to tilt it, <kbd>Z</kbd> and <kbd>X</kbd> to turn it, and <kbd>R</kbd> to level it.
5. Click **Stop** when you are done.

![A drone placed and flying in the editor](images/editor_fly_props.png)

**It did not fly?** Read the message at the top of the **Inspector**: it says why. If you have more than one drone, select the one to fly first. **Run** flies the selected drone.
