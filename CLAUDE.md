# Spade — what is expected of any agent working here

- **Stay in your realm.** Work is split into realms (Core, Physics, Rendering, Interface, Test/Docs) coordinated by a lead session; take cross-realm questions to the lead.
- **Nothing leaves this box without the owner's direct word.** No session pushes on its own.
- **Keep it general and deterministic.** Nothing in Spade is specific to one project that builds on it, and the same seed and inputs give the same results.
- **Judgment over literal rule-following.** If a guideline doesn't fit the situation, say so and do the sensible thing.
- **Make changes easy to review.** Small, clear diffs, with a short note on what changed and why.

[`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md) covers the repo and [`docs/design/`](docs/design/) the design record. Open the section a task needs (`grep -n '^#'`).

The rules for every project are `~/.claude/agent-tree/RULES.md`, with `JOBS.md` beside it.
